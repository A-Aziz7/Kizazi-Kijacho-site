Privacy, legal notice and accessibility

Three statements Swiss law requires of a site like this one, adapted from the University of Zurich's own data protection statement where relevant.

Privacy policy

This statement explains what data this website collects, why, and what rights you have — in line with the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (in force since 1 September 2023) and adapted from the University of Zurich's own data protection statement.

Controller and contact

This site is operated by the Kizazi Kijacho research program, Department of Economics, University of Zurich, Schönberggasse 1, 8001 Zürich, Switzerland. For any question about this policy or your data, contact kizazikijacho@econ.uzh.ch.

What is collected, and why

Standard access data (IP address, requested page, date and time, browser type) are logged automatically by our hosting provider, GitHub Inc., to keep the site secure and running. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visit counts — a cookie-free tool that does not collect personal data or track individuals across sites. If you email us, we process your message and address solely to reply to you.

Recipients and transfers abroad

Two providers based in the United States are involved in running this site: GitHub Inc. (hosting) and Google LLC / YouTube (the embedded field videos, which may set their own cookies — governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours). Such transfers require an appropriate safeguard under Swiss law; the university's Legal Services and Data Protection department is confirming the specific mechanism relied on for this site, since it involves external providers rather than UZH's own servers.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own cookies. Our analytics tool, Cloudflare Web Analytics, is cookie-free and does not track visitors individually, so no consent banner is required. See Cookie settings below for details.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or ask further questions, by writing to kizazikijacho@econ.uzh.ch. You may also contact the University's Legal Services and Data Protection department directly: Künstlergasse 15, CH-8001 Zurich — www.rud.uzh.ch/en.

Retention and version

Access logs and analytics data are retained according to GitHub's and Cloudflare's own data retention policies, not set by us directly. Contact emails are kept only as long as needed to respond, unless a longer period is legally required.

Research participant data from the Dodoma trial is governed by the study's own ethics approvals and consent forms, not by this policy. The two should not be confused.

Accessibility statement

The Disability Discrimination Act (BehiG) binds public bodies, and the Swiss standard eCH-0059 v3 sets the technical bar at WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Standard applied

eCH-0059 v3, WCAG 2.1 level AA. This site is partially conformant — see known exceptions below.

Known exceptions

The embedded YouTube videos on the Overview and Media pages do not currently have captions or a text transcript. The PDF outputs on the Outputs page have not yet been checked for screen-reader accessibility (tagged headings, reading order, alt text on any embedded charts or images).

Report a barrier

Email kizazikijacho@econ.uzh.ch and we will do our best to address it.

Applied across this build: body text and captions meet 4.5:1 contrast against their backgrounds. Status is always carried by a word, never colour alone. Footer and body links are underlined rather than distinguished by colour alone. Every photograph carries alternative text describing its content.

Cookie settings

This site does not set cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not collect personal data or track you individually — so there's nothing here for you to opt in or out of. The only cookies you might encounter come from the embedded YouTube videos on the Overview and Media pages, which are set by Google, not by us; see our Privacy Policy above for details.
