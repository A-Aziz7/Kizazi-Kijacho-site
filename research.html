Randomised evaluations of interventions delivered at scale

Parenting support and cash transfers in Dodoma — Analysis

A cluster-randomised trial in over 3,500 households, combined with lab-in-the-field experiments, testing a community-based parenting intervention and unconditional cash transfers — alone and in combination — on child development, and examining the importance of financial constraints, parental preferences and household decision-making.

Design
Cluster RCT with lab-in-the-field
Sample
3,566 households
Location
Dodoma region, 390 villages
Years
2022 — 2024
