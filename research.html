Kizazi Kijacho randomised controlled trial — Dodoma In progress

A cluster-randomised trial testing three approaches to supporting families through the first 1,000 days: a parenting programme delivered by community health workers using a digital app, an unconditional cash transfer, and the two combined — alongside a control group.

Design
Cluster RCT, 5 arms
Sample
3,585 households
Location
Dodoma region, 387 communities
Years
Oct 2022 — Feb 2025

Measuring preferences, constraints and decision-making.

Targeting meets constraints: cash transfers, digital access and women's empowerment Analysis

In the cash transfer arms, recipient mothers could receive funds via mobile money or nominate someone else to receive it on their behalf. This study shows that women with limited digital access are substantially more likely to nominate others — often shifting effective control over the money away from the woman the transfer was meant to reach.

Design
Sub-study within the RCT
Sample
1,239 households
Location
Dodoma region
Years
2022 — 2025

Read the working paper →

Father engagement and support to mothers: the policy elasticity of child-related spending Analysis

A lab-in-the-field allocation experiment where parents divide an endowment between themselves, their spouse, and their child. The parenting and cash-transfer arms increase fathers' allocations to the child, closing the mother–father gap in child-related spending seen in the control group.

Design
Lab-in-the-field, embedded in the RCT
Sample
1,330 parents of children under 2
Location
Dodoma region
Years
2022 — 2025

Implementation

Delivery, cost and sustainability of the interventions.

Implementation and sustainability of the parenting intervention Complete

Qualitative research combining a process evaluation, an endline evaluation, and a sustainability/scale-up study conducted 9–12 months after programme support ended. Explains how (and why) the interventions changed caregiving practice, and what helped or hindered community health workers' delivery.

Design
Qualitative — interviews & focus groups
Sample
NGOs, gov't, health workers, caregivers
Location
Dodoma region
Years
2022 — 2026

Costing study In progress

Estimating the cost per child of each intervention arm, to inform government and donor decisions on which model(s) to scale. Led by Georgina Rawle and Sadashiv Nayanpally (Oxford Policy Management).

Measuring early childhood development at scale Analysis

Developing and validating measurement tools that governments can use to track child development outcomes without specialist assessors.
